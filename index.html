How to play

GIVE OR RECEIVE

On your turn, you privately see a pleasure card for your partner. There are 5 levels of pleasure. You start at level 1.

You then have two choices:

GIVE offers them that pleasure.
or
TAKE IT FURTHER burns that pleasure card in return for getting a next level card but risks a bust.
During the 3-second burn you can tap BOTTLE IT to keep your current card.

Each higher level increases the chance of a bust.
TEMPT 5%, NAUGHTY 15%, HOT 30%, ALL IN 50%.

Your partner sees the offer without its level or value and chooses RECEIVE or SORRY, NOT MY THING.

If they choose SORRY, NOT MY THING, no one gets any points, so choose wisely on what you GIVE.

In Classic you get +1 point for pressing RECEIVE. Completing the challenge banks the giver that pleasure card’s value.

If you bust in Classic, you can choose YOU GET ZIP (lose your points) or YOU MUST STRIP (remove clothing but save points). This saves 1 point on TEMPT/NAUGHTY and 2 points on HOT/ALL IN. You have to remove the same amount of clothing as points you’re saving.

Classic: race to 20 points. The other player gets a Last Chance turn. A tie goes to Quick Strip.

Quick Strip: First player naked wins. If you bust, put 1 item of clothing back on and your turn ends.
Some Pleasure Cards make the giver remove clothing; others make the receiver put clothing back on. The amount follows the card level: TEASE 1, TEMPT 1, NAUGHTY 2, HOT 2, ALL IN 3 items.

Either person can stop or change their mind at any time. RECEIVE means happy to try — not a promise to finish.
